01 · About

Offensive mindset.
Technical foundation.

I build my cybersecurity practice around understanding how systems can be attacked, tested and strengthened. My strongest direction is Red Team / Offensive Security, supported by Linux administration, networking and web security testing. I also have training exposure to SOC technologies such as FortiSIEM and FortiAnalyzer, giving me a complementary view of how security teams monitor, detect and investigate suspicious activity.

My approach is practical: understand the environment, identify weaknesses, test them safely in an authorized scope, document the findings, and communicate the security impact clearly.
